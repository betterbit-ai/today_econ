# Keep the primary farmland subject through image selection

## Situation

The normal economy run 38997381035 selected a farmland survey/trading article. Editorial preparation passed, but the general event subject inherited the headline opening about land prices and image selection returned unmapped. The token was valid; no Instagram upload was attempted.

## What we learned

A generic first-words subject can lose a later explicit primary noun in a short headline. Preserve the canonical farmland subject only when the general event headline itself identifies a farmland survey, transaction, loan, ownership or conditions story. Read images from the locked frame; incidental body mentions must not override market or legislation frames. A mapping needs matching reviewed artwork, not a generic finance or apartment substitute.

## Next time

Replay the real headline as a regression, verify opposing locked frames, and add at least two conceptual project-owned variants for a newly supported topic. Keep manifest hashes, provenance, portrait dimensions, duplicate avoidance and actual cover visual QA. Action success before media upload is not publishing evidence.
