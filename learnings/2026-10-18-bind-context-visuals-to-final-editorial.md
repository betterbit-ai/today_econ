# Bind contextual visuals to the final editorial across every gate

## Situation

Generated assets had valid rights and broad topic tags but could pair a presidential statement with a gavel. The model image keyword was dropped during assembly, broad legacy frames overrode the final copy, and generated fallback paths escaped actual-pixel review. Requiring exact documentary depiction then blocked all three actual previews, including appropriate person-free housing illustrations.

## What we learned

A licensed or project-owned image is not automatically relevant. Context illustrations should support the headline's actual event without pretending to be event photography. Do not require exact crowds, buildings, people or leaked documents in symbolic art. Political statements and legal document examination need separate asset scopes. Use one resolved event kind for both topic and asset filtering, or incidental words such as interest rates/North Korea can make the new assets unreachable. Selection and final quality recheck must both receive the same final headline/caption. Generated fallback, rendered cover, exact MP4 bytes and stored Release identity all require consistent evidence.

## Next time

Preserve editorial search keywords end to end; test broad/mistaken historical frames; review generated pixels and final cover; include negative symbol cases and positive contextual-art cases in live verification. Record model availability failures separately from semantic rejection. Do not ship on a green workflow when every case in its report is blocked. Audio metadata checks remain distinct from actual listening.
