# Viral reach is not subscription intent

## Situation

A seven-second public-interest Reel looped well and exceeded 100,000 views, but
only 11 viewers followed the account. Shares were much higher than saves and the
profile description remained a generic news-magazine statement.

## What we learned

A timely individual story answers “why watch this now,” not “why follow this
account tomorrow.” Follow conversion needs a coherent account-level benefit,
but the internal production constraint (such as exactly three caption sentences)
is not itself an appealing audience promise. Reach, retention, saves, profile
visits, and follows must be read as separate stages rather than one success metric.

## Next time

Keep the cover focused on the headline. Build conversion through a benefit-led
profile, pinned onboarding and evergreen posts, repeatable editorial series, and
Story highlights. Compare follows per reached account over a fixed window instead
of adding production-format copy to every Reel.
