# DIEM original audio declaration

The six audio recordings in this directory were generated specifically for
DIEM by `scripts/generate-diem-audio.js`. They contain no third-party samples,
performances, melodies, or recordings.

The DIEM project owner may reproduce, edit, encode, publish, and commercially
use these recordings with DIEM content on Instagram and other DIEM-owned
channels. No rights are granted for third parties to redistribute these files
as a standalone music library.

The source script, manifest entry, and SHA-256 value together identify the
recording used for each publication.
